# SkillsAtlas pitch contents — team research pack

**This is not a designed deck.** No PPTX, no layout, no fonts. It is the *argument*: what each slide must say, why it is there, who researches the gaps, and a working four-minute speech. After research comes back, we combine this into one picture and write the final spoken text.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |

## How to use this file

1. Read **mentor notes** and **what judges will ask** first.
2. Pick a slide or a research box. **Decide who does what as a team** — this pack does not assign people.
3. Bring back: a headline, a proof (source + date), and anything you cannot prove so we cut it.
4. Then we combine into one picture and write the spoken text. Do not design yet.

**Numbers rule:** the deck may quote only from `docs/evidence-base.md`. If your research finds a better number, add it there with source and date *before* it goes on a slide. Invented TAM, invented savings, invented “we improve job outcomes” = we lose Q&A.

**Persona numbers** (2,400 SKUs, SAP) are from the synthetic CV. Say them only while that line is on screen. They are demo colour, not Ireland statistics.

### Example — use AI as a research assistant (not as the author)

You pick Slide 6 (traction / conversations). Copy that slide’s “on the slide” lines, the evidence rules, and the research box. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor:

> I am researching Slide 6 of a TechIreland pitch. Mentors said open conversations with potential clients are a judging bonus. Find 3 small or mid-tier Irish employment / ETB / outplacement contacts we could actually call this week. For each: organisation, rôle, public phone or email, and why they fit small/mid-tier. Do not invent a quote. Do not invent that we already spoke to them. Do not add statistics that are not in this pack.

Then you do the human part: call one of them. Log date, what they said, whether we may name them. **That log is what goes on the slide.** The AI list is not traction. A conversation is.

## What mentors asked us to land

Treat these as brief for the live ten. If a slide fights this section, the slide is wrong.

**Open conversations with potential clients.** A mentor said this is a huge bonus for judging. A working demo proves you can build. A real conversation proves the problem exists outside the team. Start named conversations this week. Log date, role, what they said. "In conversation with..." beats a fake logo wall. Use one mentorship slot on concept + pitch format, not extra features.

**Scalability — how 10 becomes 200.** Not a national launch. Copy the sitting. Each gate has a kill-point. Slide 8. Numbers are a plan, not traction.

**Defensibility — why others will not just build it.** Large enterprises can — they have internal AI teams. We do not fight them. Beachhead: small and mid-tier providers whose infrastructure is outdated (course list, spreadsheet, three-week gap). Moat is the sitting plus the gates, not a patent. Never say "nobody else can build this." Slides 5 and 7.

## What judges score — and the three things that must be unforgettable

Organisers and the challenge playbook are consistent: **problem, demo, team**. The catalogue also rewards end-to-end workflow, measurable value, hand-off, traceability, responsible data use.

If a judge repeats one line to another judge, it should be this:

> They have already been assessed. We turn what they have actually done into two reachable roles and a board they walk out holding. The system proposes; the person decides. Every line on that board traces to a confirmed claim.

**Three visible moments** (engineer these; do not hope they appear):

1. **Confirm / skip** — nothing used until they say it is true. Skip is free.
2. **Weak line becomes evidence** — “managed stock” → number + tool + date.
3. **Leave with the board** — STAR + predicted questions; flip interview setting; stories stay; questions change. WhatsApp is bonus, not the close.

If the model is down: `?replay=golden`. Do not narrate a spinner.

## What judges will ask (prepare these before the deck is pretty)

These are the likely questions. Short answers only. Proof sits on a screen or in `docs/evidence-base.md`.

### They will ask these. Ranked.

**1. Who is this for, this week?**  
A worker whose role is gone or about to go — and the adviser who sees them about every three weeks. Visible cases this quarter: Meta / Covalen (~700 contractor roles; `evidence-base.md`). **Research still owed:** one named, reachable adviser or outplacement contact. Kill-sheet item 1.

**2. Is this just ChatGPT on a CV?**  
No. ChatGPT gives keywords and a course list — that *is* the broken handoff. We confirm evidence, cap questions, pin skills to **ESCO**, return **exactly two** reachable routes, never rank the person, and refuse to print a story we cannot trace.

**3. Is it actually an agent, or a chat UI?**  
One agent with tools, wrapped in rules. Parse and explain are model calls. Scoring, ESCO GET, reachability, course join, never-invent, privacy payload are **code**. BPMN in `docs/bpmn/` is the picture. We do not demo a multi-agent swarm.

**4. Who decides — the model or the human?**  
The worker confirms, skips, and chooses the route. The adviser sits with them. The agent does not pick a winner and does not infer seniority, age, health, or “employability.”

**5. What about GDPR / sensitive data?**  
Demo uses **synthetic** personas. Schema cannot hold protected traits. Partner (if on) gets four fields only; nothing said there returns to scoring. Compliance is in the architecture, not a policy PDF.

**6. Why Ireland first? Who pays?**  
Activation already exists (Intreo → contracted providers). Appointments have gaps. Buyers are providers / ETBs / employer outplacement — not a consumer download. **Do not** quote JobPath fee ladders on stage until they live in `evidence-base.md` with a current source. The 2019 Dáil schedule is research, not a slide yet.

**7. What’s your traction?**  
Honest: two-week PoC. Live URL. Four screens. Guardrail tests. No paying customers, no signed office LOI. Traction *for this room* is a working demo plus a named next conversation — not fake ARR.

**8. What if the model invents a story?**  
Never-invent gate: interview-pack claims must point at `CONFIRMED` evidence. Fail → strip and log. That is a tested rule, not a promise.

**9. Why only two routes?**  
A job board is the old product. Two reachable paths force a human choice. Ranking ten people or ten jobs is the thing we refuse.

**10. What’s the ask?**  
Hub: go through to Galway. Galway / mentors: one introduction to a provider or ETB adult-guidance desk for a **one-cohort pilot** (synthetic-first, then a small real sitting). Not “partners and funding” in the abstract.

**11. How do you get to 200 users?**  
10 in one sitting → 20 as one office cohort → 200 by copying the sitting to more small/mid-tier offices. Kill-points between gates. Not ads. (Slide 8. Proposed.)

**12. Why won’t a bank just build this?**  
They can. They have internal AI teams. We sell to small and mid-tier providers whose systems are still a printout. (Slides 5 and 7.)

### Language we will not use

- “We autonomously redeploy people.”
- “We improve motivation / predict who will disengage.”
- “We assess employability.”
- “No risk” (WhatsApp or otherwise).
- “Platform” before they have seen the board.
- Market size as the opening.

Say instead: *we make the responsible human faster, better evidenced, and able to leave with proof.*

## The ten slides

Each slide: **one bold idea**, then almost no text. Anything in *italics* is a working line — replace only with a sourced better line. Who takes which slide: decide as a team.

### Slide 1 — Hook

**Idea:** They have already been assessed. We give them evidence they can carry.

**On the slide (max):**

- Title: **SkillsAtlas**
- Line: *The evidence agent for the silent weeks after a redundancy assessment.*
- Sub: *Same CV. Confirmed evidence. Two reachable roles. They leave with the board.*
- Footer: TechIreland National AI Challenge 2026 · Hub 14 Sep

**Why this slide:** Judges decide in seconds whether this is “another careers chatbot.” The hook must contain the **old handoff** (assessed) and the **artefact** (the board). Mentor: CV origin starts here, not on slide 4.

**Do not:** logo soup, team names, tech stack, “AI-powered platform.”

**Research to bring back:** final one-liner (problem owner + presenter). Current locked sentence is in `docs/evidence-base.md` (Proposed). Shorten it until it fits one breath.

### Slide 2 — Problem

**Idea:** Ireland assesses, prints a course list, and leaves them alone for ~three weeks.

**On the slide (max):**

- Headline: *You’ve been assessed. Here’s a course list. See you in three weeks.*
- Three beats: **Assessed → printout → silent weeks**
- **One** number, sourced, e.g. *ICT employment, under-30s, 2023–25: −33%* (Government analysis via Bloomberg/LA Times, Jul 2026) **or** *~700 Covalen roles* — pick **one**. Caption the source in 8pt.
- Optional small: *Appointments about every three weeks. Nothing sits with them in between.*

**Why this slide:** This is the highest-weighted judge lens (problem + buyer). Production plan: **the one real number lives here.** Mentor: “Assessed” must be visible as a beat.

**Do not:** five statistics. Do not put 2,400 SKUs here. Do not blame workers.

**Research to bring back (problem owner):**

- [ ] Confirm which **single** number we quote. Must already be in `evidence-base.md`.
- [ ] One sentence from a real adviser (even anonymised) about the three-week gap — or we stay with public cadence, not a fake quote.
- [ ] Named person / office we can actually call (kill-sheet 1–2).

### Slide 4 — How the agents work

**Idea:** One agent, tools, and hard gates — not a chat that “just knows.”

**On the slide (max):**

- Headline: *The model drafts. Rules decide what is allowed. The person confirms.*
- Three columns:

| AI (cheap, then one reasoning call) | Code (always) | Human |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parse CV · ask 2–3 questions · explain a route · draft STAR | Score evidence · ESCO pin · two-route rule · never-invent · no protected traits | Confirm / correct / skip · pick a route · pick interview setting |

- Footer: *~6 model calls per journey. Guardrail on every AI output. Fail → regenerate once → drop the claim.*

**Why this slide:** 2026 theme is agentic workflows with hand-off and traceability. This is also how we beat “why not ChatGPT.” Role decomposition is **conceptual** (challenge rule: no heavy multi-agent). Picture: `docs/bpmn/skillsatlas-4-screens.bpmn`.

**Do not:** Kubernetes, model IDs from memory, a spaghetti agent diagram, “our proprietary LLM.”

**Research to bring back (builder / BPMN owner):**

- [ ] Screenshot of the four-row BPMN that matches the **built** demo (reconcile before 13 Sep).
- [ ] One line on which steps are live vs labelled simulated — judges ask.

## Working four-minute speech

Spoken, human, works **with or without** slides. Time on a clock. After research returns, the presenter cuts this to the final freeze — do not invent a second speech in a side chat.

**~0:00–0:35 · Hook + problem**

Ireland already does the first half of this job. Someone is assessed. They get a course list. Next sitting is about three weeks away. In that gap the plan lives only in their head.

We are not replacing that meeting. We take the **same CV** into the next sitting — the file the adviser already has, or the one they bring. Nothing on it is used until they say: this is true.

**~0:35–1:10 · Solution**

SkillsAtlas is an evidence agent. It turns what they have actually done into two roles they can reach, a plan that points at a real Skillnet, SOLAS, or Springboard+ course, and proof they can carry into an interview.

We do not rank them. We do not decide who is redeployed. We propose. They choose.

**~1:10–2:10 · How it works (and the demo beat)**

A model reads the CV. That is the cheap, small job. Then the person confirms. Watch the weak line: “managed stock.” Two questions. Skip is always allowed. When they answer, it becomes a number, a tool, a date. That is evidence. That is not a keyword.

Rules sit around every model call: no protected traits, cap the questions, pin skills to ESCO — the European skills language — and if a story cannot trace to a confirmed line, we strip it. The interesting part is not the model. It is the trail.

**~2:10–2:50 · Two routes + board**

Exactly two reachable routes. Not a job board. Not “you would be hired.” What already transfers. What is missing. A real Irish course for the gap. They pick.

They leave holding a cheat-board: STAR stories and predicted questions. They tell us who is in the room — a phone screen is not a hiring-manager panel. Same evidence, different questions. Every line traces to something they confirmed.

**~2:50–3:25 · Ireland, money, honesty**

This is Ireland first because the sitting already exists. The user is the worker. The buyer is the small or mid-tier office that already gets paid to stay with them — not a large enterprise with an internal AI team, and not a newly redundant person buying a subscription.

What we have today is a two-week proof: four screens, a live URL, tests on the guardrails. We do not have paying seats and we will not pretend we do. Ten people in one sitting. Twenty when that office copies the next week. Two hundred when other small offices copy the sitting. If the first ten do not come back holding a board, we stop.

**~3:25–4:00 · Team + ask**

We are a challenge team that can show this cold, in ninety seconds, with a recording in the next tab if the wifi dies.

On the fourteenth we are asking to go to Galway. After that we are asking for one introduction — one adviser, one cohort — to test a simple measure: do they come back to the next appointment holding a board they wrote from their own evidence.

That’s the product. Not a chatbot. A trail they can trust, and something they walk out holding.

1. Team decides who takes which slide. Paste **Research to bring back** under that slide (or the conversation log).

3. Only then: layout. If a claim has no source, it is deleted, not typeset smaller.
4. Read the four-minute speech out loud on a clock. Cut anything that needs a footnote.
5. Upload before **13 Sep 2pm**.
