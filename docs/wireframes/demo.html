Ireland already assesses people, then hands them a list. This sitting turns the same CV into evidence, a named gap, two jobs they can reach, and a board they walk out holding.

For the person whose job is gone or about to go, in a small employment-office sitting. The person does not pay. Practice CV — not a real worker. We propose. They confirm, skip, and choose. We do not say you would be hired.

  1. Confirm the lines — nothing is used until they say yes.
  2. Answer or Skip two questions. Skip is free. No penalty.
  3. Pick one of two jobs. Flip who is in the interview. Then save the board as PDF.
