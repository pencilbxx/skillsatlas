# Partner rooms — same clicks, different sentence

Not customers. Not logos for the deck. Context for the sitting we wrap, and for a later pivot if the first office is the wrong door.

**This fortnight:** one demo, one URL, four screens. No APIs into their systems. Swap only who sits, which `data/courses/` row (if the gap matches), and the one spoken sentence. Do not add screens, fields, or a second product per room.

**Hard line:** do not claim Turas Nua, FIT, ReBOOT, or Back to Work Connect as customers. A local office or programme can still be the beachhead. Traction is a named conversation, not their brand on a slide.

---

## Same process in every room

1. Problem — they already assessed; the person leaves with a list.
2. Confirm what they did (skip is free). Two or three questions that strengthen weak evidence.
3. Exactly two reachable roles. Real Irish course only if it closes a named gap.
4. They walk out holding the board. Their jobs board, appointments, 12-week course, or vacancy list stay theirs.

Never: ranking the person, protected-trait fields, a SWOT/personality intake, inventing a story about a career break, replacing their catalogue with 200 courses.

## The four rooms

### 1. Turas Nua (Intreo Partner sitting)

**What they already have:** the assessment, the appointment book, TN Connect / barriers work, a job board. Cadence is roughly every three weeks. The silent weeks are the hole.

**How it feels:**

> You already assessed them. We take that CV, confirm the work, give two roles they can reach, and a board for the weeks until you see them again. Your job board and your appointments stay yours.

**Wrap, do not replace:** TN Connect, vacancies, three-week diary.

**Why this is the default beachhead:** the problem slide is this sitting. Live Register scale (see below) belongs in the *problem*, not as a pitch to the CSO.

**Pivot away if:** they hear “we replace Intreo Partners.” Then say FIT or ReBOOT — same demo, different one-liner.

Public context: contracted Intreo Partner (NES / LAES), with Seetec as the other large brand. Sources live in `docs/evidence-base.md`. Do not invent contract values or office internals.

### 2. FIT (fast track into tech)

**What they already have:** ICT courses, apprenticeships, employer placement. They are the pipeline, not an activation office.

**How it feels:**

> You already have the tech pipeline. We do not pick from 200 courses. We confirm what they did, show two reachable roles, and if the gap is digital we name one FIT path. They leave with a board, not a prospectus.

**Wrap, do not replace:** their course list or placement. At most one FIT row in `data/courses/` when the gap is digital.

**Pivot toward if:** the judge or office cares about *which course*, not the three-week gap. Still two ESCO routes first; the course is the gap closer, not the product.

Site: [fit.ie](https://www.fit.ie)

### 3. Women ReBOOT (Digital Skillnet / TU Dublin)

**What they already have:** a ~12-week returner programme (training + placement). The hole they name is the interview — proving the work they already did, not another prospectus.

**How it feels:**

> You already have training and a placement. The hole you named is the interview. We confirm the work they already have (no extra story about the break), two honest next roles, and the board they take into the panel. Your 12 weeks stay yours.

**Wrap, do not replace:** the programme, SWOT/competency worksheets, or their placement. Do not copy ReBOOT intake into the UI (`docs/bridget-hr-feedback.md`).

**Pivot toward if:** the sitting is return-to-work / panel, not Live Register activation. Schema still has no gender, age, or “career break” fields. Confirm the CV; do not infer the break.

HR examples from Brigitte inform copy and the deck only — not a second product.

### 4. Back to Work Connect

**What they already have:** a private jobs + courses platform for midlife returners, plus returner-friendly employers. Marketplace, not Intreo.

**How it feels:**

> You already have the jobs board, the course list, and returner-friendly employers. We do not replace that marketplace. We take the CV, confirm the work they already did (no extra story about the break), two reachable roles, and a board they take to the employer you already introduced. Your listings and your directory stay yours.

**Wrap, do not replace:** their vacancy list or course directory. Same spine as ReBOOT (prove the work for the interview) plus a bit of Turas Nua (they keep the jobs).

**Pivot toward if:** the buyer is a private returner platform, not a contracted PES office. Still one URL beside their stack. No scrape of their board this fortnight.

Site: [backtoworkconnect.ie](https://backtoworkconnect.ie)

## Not a fifth room — CSO Live Register

[Live Register August 2026](https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-lr/liveregisteraugust2026/) is a monthly count. **Do not pitch SkillsAtlas to the CSO.** Quote it on the problem slide so the sitting has a size. Numbers and sourcing: `docs/evidence-base.md`.

Do not put CSO age, sex, or nationality tables into the product or the demo. Those are statistics, not SkillsAtlas fields.

Problem-slide line (not a sales line to Cork):

> 182,517 people were on the Live Register in August 2026. Most of them for under a year. Activation already exists for tens of thousands more. They are already counted. They are already assessed. They still leave with a list. We turn the same CV into two roles they can reach and a board they walk out holding.
