# Brand — SkillsAtlas

This file controls public names, visual tokens, and final-artefact terminology for the live prototype, film, and Hub deck.

## Names

| What | Name | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | **SkillsAtlas** | Hub slides, live URL, Interview Board PDF, film |
| Final worker artefact | **SkillsAtlas Interview Board** | Screen 04, slide and spoken copy, PDF filename and header |
| Challenge team | **RedeployMate** | Secondary prototype attribution and footer; never part of the product title |

Do not use **RedeployMate (Skills Atlas)**, **cheat-board**, **evidence pack**, or **board they wrote** as public product names.

## Core public line

> **A list gives you options. SkillsAtlas gives you proof.**

The public problem is **title without proof**, not a universal claim that an employment office abandons people for three weeks.

## Live URL

https://skillsatlas.vercel.app — clickable prototype in `docs/wireframes/`. It is not a live production AI pipeline.

A judge opening the URL cold lands on **00 Start**. The sitting remains Screens 01–04. On stage, the clicker starts on Screen 02.

## Prototype language

Use:

- **clickable prototype**
- **prepared practice CV**
- **two credible role routes**
- **illustrative learning option** unless a current listing is verified and linked
- **question example** unless live generation is implemented and tested
- **worker confirms, skips, and chooses**

Do not use:

- live AI product
- production system
- real course or guaranteed place without verification
- predicted question for a fixed prepared string
- top match, ranked candidate, or hiring likelihood
