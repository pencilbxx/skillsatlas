## Sent email (9 Sep 2026)

**To:** Martina, Turas Nua Enniscorthy  
**Subject:** SkillsAtlas — follow-up from Enniscorthy

Hi Martina,

This is Andrew. Just wanted to follow you up about my project for the TechIreland AI Challenge, and that workflow I was telling you about at our last meeting.

Here is a short description of what it does. I wonder if it can be helpful or valuable for your company.

SkillsAtlas is a short sitting with a jobseeker that turns their CV into two reachable roles with proof, plus a board they can hold until the next appointment.

Everyone else hands someone a list. We hand them proof.

You already assessed them. SkillsAtlas is an AI workflow that orchestrates the next bit of that sitting. It reads the CV for real work (not just matching words) and confirms with the person what stays true. Then it asks a few sharp questions so a weak line becomes a number, a tool, or a date. From that verified picture, the AI proposes exactly two reachable roles that person can actually reach with the evidence they have. They choose which path to hold. If something still blocks that path, the AI names the gap and points to one concrete Irish course that closes it (Skillnet, SOLAS, or ETB), only when the gap is real. No course dump. They walk out holding a short board of what was confirmed, which role they picked, and any next step, for the weeks until you see them again. The intelligence sits in how those steps chain together in one pass.

Your job board and your appointments stay yours. We do not replace what you run. We make the sitting count, and the gap between sittings hold.

If useful, here is a clickable walkthrough of that flow (wireframe, not a live product yet):
https://skillsatlas.vercel.app

Let me know what you think, and whether this is something that could be implemented in your company to meet your goals and help your clients.

Andrew
