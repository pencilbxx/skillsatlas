## Angle C — title without proof (workforce transition + education)

**The problem:** workers facing redundancy can see the **job title** they should be aiming for — not the **evidence**, the **gaps**, or the **steps** that make them a credible candidate for it.

That is already our sitting. A course list, a jobs board, a redundancy pack, and “you should go for supervisor” are all **titles**. Keywords match titles. We make the person **credible** for a title: what they already did, what is still missing, one next step, a board they can stand over.

### Does this become B2C?

**No. Not necessary.**

| | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **User** | The worker. They feel this problem. |
| **Buyer** | Still the room that already sits with them: outplacement, local office, ETB / Skillnet / Springboard, later a recruiter’s desk. |

The person does **not** have to download an app or pay. Redundancy is a bad moment to sell a subscription. They already have an appointment. We wrap that.

B2C would mean: they find us alone, they pay. Possible later. Not required for this angle. User ≠ buyer stays.

Education fits the same problem without becoming a student app: the catalogue is titles (and course names). We do not dump 200 courses. We say whether this person is credible for that aim, and name **one** course only if it closes a real gap.

### One-breath if C is the hook

> Everyone else hands you a title. We hand you proof.  
> After redundancy you can see the job you are supposed to want. You cannot see the evidence, the gap, or the next step.  
> Same CV: they confirm what they did, we name what is missing, they pick a path they can actually stand over, and they walk out interview-ready.

**B — Recruiter tool.** This is the CRM pain she actually described. Walkthrough below.

### B — how a recruiter would use it

Picture her example. The agency is trying to fill a **systems administrator**. LinkedIn Recruiter already ate the email quota. Zero replies. So they go back to **their own CRM** — people they already hold.

Today: they open CV after CV. A file says “systems administrator.” Inside it might only be a **course**, not the job. They cannot see that until they read it. Two hours. Still no one. Time is money.

**What the evidence agent does (desk, recruiter alone):**

1. Recruiter names the live job (or pastes the spec).
2. They point it at CVs they already have — not a new job board.
3. The agent reads each CV for **evidence of the work**, not the job title.
4. It flags the lie: *title says systems admin; the only line is training.* Another file has a tool, a date, a scale — that is evidence.
5. Recruiter still **chooses who to call**. We do not rank “best human.” We stop them opening forty PDFs.

That is the CRM fix: **search what you already have, without drowning.** LinkedIn finds strangers. This opens the drawer.

They have not spoken to the person yet. So **the recruiter does not answer our extra questions.** If they did, they would be guessing. That is inventing. The agent can only say: *this line is weak — ask this on the call.*

**What happens when they get the candidate on the phone (or in the room):**

Now it looks like the sitting we already built.

- **Who answers the extra questions?** The **candidate**. Always. “Did you run the box, or only sit the course?”
- **Who gets the STAR board?** Both, same PDF.
  - The **candidate** walks into the client’s interview holding it (and the AI helper).
  - The **recruiter** attaches it when they put the person in front of the **client** (the employer who pays the agency). It is the pack: *this is what we can stand over — not a keyword.*

So B is two beats, not one app screen:

| Beat | Who is there | What the agent does | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Desk | Recruiter only | Evidence vs keyword on CVs they already have | Who is worth a call. Weak lines marked. |
| 2. Sitting | Recruiter + candidate | Confirm, questions, two honest paths if needed, STAR + helper | One board. Recruiter submits it. Candidate interviews with it. |

Beat 1 solves the rabbit hole. Beat 2 is our current demo. A Hub click that only shows beat 2 is **A**. A Hub click that only shows beat 1 is a different screen (job + one CV → evidence, no STAR yet).
