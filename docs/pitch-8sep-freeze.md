## What we actually locked

The sitting is the product. Four steps on stage = four screens:

1. Confirm — their CV. Only keep what they say is true. Skip is free.
2. Two or three questions. A weak line becomes a story (number, tool, date).
3. Two jobs they can actually reach. They pick. One real Irish course if something is missing.
4. They walk out holding a board.

We wrap the office. We do not replace their meetings or their job board.

**Hook (one breath):** Everyone else hands you a list. We hand you proof.

**Buyer (one breath):** We don’t replace what you run. We make the weeks between appointments count.

**Who pays:** the small employment office. The person does not pay.

**Name:** SkillsAtlas until the team votes otherwise. Do not put RedeployMate on Hub slides.

## Group answers (so Q&A does not reopen the product)

1. **Full assessment as a pre-step?** No. Confirm + 1–2 questions (cap 3) *in* their sitting.
2. **Flex questions if the CV is thin?** Yes, inside the cap. No deep at-home pass this fortnight.
3. **Two jobs on screen?** Yes. Rank 5–10 on screen / “show more”? No. Silent fallback if matching fails: already planned.
4. **UVP + buyer gain?** Yes. List vs proof. Time back. Something in the gap. No promised ROI.
5. **B2B, Turas Nua / Seetec?** Yes to office-first. Turas Nua = context, not a logo. Seetec = next office of the same type. A conversation is not a contract.
6. **Orchestration now, fine-tune later?** Yes as a story: one agent, tools, person confirms. Fine-tune only after real outcomes. Do not say we fine-tuned. Do not say LangGraph unless we actually use it.
