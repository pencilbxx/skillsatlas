# Brand — SkillsAtlas

Names and look for the live sitting and the silent film. Tokens match `docs/explainer-remotion/` and `docs/wireframes/demo.css`.

## Names

| What | Name | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | **SkillsAtlas** | Hub slides, live URL, PDF header, film |
| Challenge team | **RedeployMate** | Prototype chip, footer, PDF header as team — not the product title |

Do not title the product “RedeployMate (Skills Atlas)”. Hub slides say SkillsAtlas only (`docs/pitch-8sep-freeze.md`).

## Live URL

https://skillsatlas.vercel.app — clickable sitting (`docs/wireframes/`). Not the Next.js app. Public, no password.

Judges who open the URL cold land on **00 Start** (who we are, optional silent film, shoreline). The sitting they click is still **01–04**. Hub live click does not tour 00; the clicker starts on evidence or the board (`docs/hub-rundown.md`).
