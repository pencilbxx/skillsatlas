# SkillsAtlas host rooms — same four screens, different professional

This file describes possible **settings**, not customers, logos, integrations, or traction. The canonical public story remains `docs/pitch-slide-text.md`.

## Same process in both active rooms

1. A worker or candidate brings a CV.
2. They confirm or skip the original claims.
3. They answer at most two or three focused questions and separately confirm any stronger wording.
4. They choose between exactly two credible, unranked role routes.
5. They leave with a route-specific SkillsAtlas Interview Board saved as PDF.

The professional may guide and click. The worker answers, confirms, skips, and chooses. The organisation hosting the sitting is the proposed payer; the worker does not pay.

## Room A — redundancy or transition sitting

| Element | Current position |
| --- | --- |
| Who is present | Worker + employment or outplacement adviser |
| What stays with the host | Its appointments, case management, job board, employer relationships, and learning catalogue |
| What SkillsAtlas adds | A structured confirmation conversation, two constrained routes, a named gap, and an Interview Board |
| What remains open | Whether an external tool fits the workflow, privacy and security requirements, buyer authority, and willingness to pay |

The 10 Sep Enniscorthy conversation is useful field evidence, not a customer or pilot. It supported evidence beyond keywords and two constrained choices. It did not prove that the office is broken, that appointments follow a fixed three-week programme, or that a standalone external product fits its systems.

**Safe room sentence:**

> “The adviser keeps the meeting and the existing systems. SkillsAtlas helps the worker turn their own CV into confirmed evidence, choose between two credible routes, and leave with an Interview Board.”

## Room B — recruiter sitting with the candidate

| Element | Current position |
| --- | --- |
| Who is present | Candidate + recruiter at a mid-tier agency |
| What stays with the host | Its CRM, vacancies, job boards, client relationship, and hiring judgment |
| What SkillsAtlas adds | The same confirmation conversation, two constrained routes when useful, and an Interview Board the candidate can use |
| What remains open | Whether this sitting saves enough time, who has buying authority, how candidate consent works, and what integrations or controls are required |

This is **not** a recruiter-alone search tool. The candidate answers questions and confirms the evidence. The recruiter does not answer on the candidate’s behalf.

**Safe room sentence:**

> “The recruiter keeps the CRM, vacancies, and judgment. With the candidate present, SkillsAtlas confirms the evidence behind the CV and produces an Interview Board both can stand over.”

## Parked settings

FIT, Women ReBOOT, Back to Work Connect, ETBs, unions, and regional workforce programmes may be useful later discovery settings. They are not part of the Hub product story and should not appear as customers, traction, or extra market slides.

The CSO Live Register is context, not a customer or product input. Do not add age, sex, nationality, health, or other protected-trait fields to SkillsAtlas.

## Separate product: recruiter CRM search

The field conversation surfaced a recruiter-alone problem: searching existing CRM records by evidence rather than titles. That would require system access, candidate-consent design, privacy and security review, integration, a different interface, and a buyer-value test. It is **not being built this week** and must not be smuggled into the four-screen demonstration.

## Non-negotiable boundary

Do not build a room-specific product, extra screen, CRM search, job board, catalogue, or ranking system before the Hub. If a setting cannot use the same four candidate-present screens, it belongs in post-challenge discovery.
